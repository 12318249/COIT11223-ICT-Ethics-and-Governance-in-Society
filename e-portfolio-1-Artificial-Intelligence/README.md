Summary of the Artefact

Ofosu-Asare (2026) provides an academically grounded look at the ways Academic institutions can establish ethical guidelines for the proper and responsible utilization of Generative Artificial Intelligence. The article uses systematic literature review to pinpoint certain ethical issues such as privacy, bias, transparency, accountability and academic integrity. It outlines the principles of AI and how many institutions have implemented their own guidelines, but tackles some of the issues of applying principles effectively in the educational setting. While restrictions are certainly part of the side effects of AI adoption, ensuring responsible adoption involves ongoing evaluation, AI literacy, and clear policies as well (p. 37), Ofosu-Asare (2026).
